Account deletion

You can request account deletion from within Accure when that option is available. You can also email support with the subject "Accure account deletion request" from the email address linked to your account.

Account deletion removes your Accure profile, saved protocols, uploaded profile image, and sign-in account where supported, unless limited records must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Accure Privacy Policy

How Accure handles app data.

This Privacy Policy explains how Accure collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes data for the Accure mobile app.

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Wellness reference

Accure provides educational acupressure information and saved protocol tools.

Firebase-backed account

Account, profile, protocol, and optional image data are handled through Firebase services.

No advertising sale

The current release is designed without data broker sharing, personal-data sales, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Data Accure may collect

Account and sign-in data

Email address, phone number, authentication provider identifiers, Firebase user ID, and provider profile details made available when you use email, phone OTP, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple on iOS.

Profile data

First name, surname, practitioner or patient selection, phone number, email address when available, account creation/update timestamps, and optional profile image.

Saved app content

Saved acupressure protocols, selected points, healing-system selections, dates, notes or labels that are part of saved protocols, and related Firestore records.

Images you choose

Profile photos or app images that you choose from your device gallery for upload. Accure does not need your full photo library for normal browsing.

Device-local data

Limited profile details may be stored on your device so the app can remember profile completion and improve the signed-in experience.

How Accure uses data

  • To create, secure, and manage your account.
  • To verify phone numbers and support email, Google, and Apple sign-in flows.
  • To save, retrieve, update, and delete your profile, protocol records, and optional profile image.
  • To provide app features for browsing acupressure points and saving protocols.
  • To prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Wellness and educational use

Accure is intended as a wellness and educational acupressure reference for practitioners and patients. The app does not provide medical diagnosis, emergency care, or a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns, treatment decisions, urgent symptoms, pregnancy-related concerns, or any condition that may require professional care.

Service providers

Accure uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage. These services help us authenticate users, store account/profile data, store saved protocols, and store optional uploaded images.

Google Sign-In is provided by Google. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple and is shown only on iOS when available. These providers may process sign-in data according to their own privacy terms.

Sharing and tracking

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your personal data with data brokers.
  • The current release is designed without third-party advertising SDKs or third-party advertising tracking.
  • We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate Accure, comply with law, protect users, or complete a request you make.

Permissions

Photo or media access is requested only when you choose to upload or change an image. You can deny or later revoke these permissions in your device settings.

Accure does not require location, contacts, microphone, calendar, or health-record access for the current release.

Retention and deletion

We retain account, profile, protocol, and image data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide Accure.

When account deletion is requested through the app or the official support channel, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account where possible, Firestore profile data, saved protocols, uploaded profile images, and device-local profile data controlled by the app, unless we must retain limited information for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete server-side account data. Use the in-app account deletion option when available or contact us through the support page.

Your choices

You may browse supported acupressure reference content as a guest where the app allows it. You can sign out at any time. You can also request access, correction, or deletion of your account data through the app or support page.

Security

We use Firebase platform security controls and reasonable technical safeguards to protect app data. No internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to keep access limited to what is needed to operate and support Accure.

Children

Accure is not directed to children under 13 or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal data through Accure, contact us through the support page so we can review and delete the data where appropriate.

International processing

Depending on where Firebase and other service providers process information, your data may be stored or processed in countries other than the country where you live. We use service providers that maintain privacy and security programs for their services.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when Accure changes, when store requirements change, or when legal requirements change. The effective date above shows when this policy was last updated.

Contact

For privacy requests, app support, account deletion, or data requests, use the Accure support page at ../support/.

Store disclosure note

This policy is intended to support Accure's Google Play Data Safety and Apple App Privacy disclosures. Those store disclosures should be kept consistent with this page and updated whenever the app adds a new SDK, permission, data type, or data use.
